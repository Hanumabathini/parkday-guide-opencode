The 64 Parks Planning Checklist is a free resource we offer by email. It is meant to sit beside the park pages on Parkday Guide and help you turn reading into a plan. This post walks through how to use it, and what it is not meant to do.

## What the checklist is for

A checklist is useful when it turns a large decision into small, answerable questions. This one follows the same three phases as the site. Planning prompts ask what you need to settle before you travel. Visit prompts ask what you want to confirm about the day in the park. After prompts ask about the gateway town and the time around the visit. You can work through it for one park or use it to compare several before you choose.

## Start with a shortlist

If you are still deciding where to go, use the checklist as a way to compare parks on equal terms. Write down your shortlist and answer the planning prompts for each one. The goal is not to produce a perfect itinerary. It is to find which parks need more preparation and which fit the trip you actually want. Note where the guide marks a page as pending verification, because that tells you where you will need to do more checking yourself.

## Move from broad to specific

Once you have a park in mind, use the checklist to gather the details that affect your dates and your route. Start with the park's page on [nps.gov](https://www.nps.gov) for current conditions, alerts and operating hours, then follow the source link on the corresponding guide page and record the retrieval date next to your answer. This habit keeps your notes honest. If you come back to the plan later, you can see which answers were current when you wrote them and which need another look.

## Keep it working

A checklist only helps if you keep it close to the decision. Print it or keep a copy where you plan, and cross items off as you confirm them. Leave space for sources, because the source matters as much as the answer. The checklist is free and sent by email, and it is a working document rather than a finished plan. The site is still a pre-launch build, and the checklist is our way of giving you a usable structure while the park pages continue to be verified.
