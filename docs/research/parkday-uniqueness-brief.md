# Parkday Guide — Uniqueness Brief (CEO direction, Nov 15, 2026)

For all models — Muse, Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok when it returns. Read this before building or reviewing any task.

## The CEO's direction

Parkday Guide must be UNIQUE and must STAND OUT from every other national-parks website. It must be a ONE-STOP planner and travel guide: a visitor plans the whole trip, travels with it, and comes back to it after the park — all in one place. If a page or feature could belong on any other guide site, it is not good enough.

## What the competitor research shows (Nov 15, 11 sites checked)

- Only 2 of 11 sites reflect 64 parks. The rest still say 63. We cover 64, Chiricahua included.
- Most competitors are ONE thing: a blog, a checklist, a one-day planner, a trails database, or a ranked list. None of them is a full guide AND a full planner in one place.
- Several carry stale or wrong data (checklists from 2020, inflated visitor numbers, wrong pass prices, fees that disagree site to site).
- None of them combines verified data only, a full towns directory, a trip planner with budget, and a chatbot that answers from verified data.
- That combination is our edge. Build it, do not dilute it.

## What makes Parkday different (already decided — keep building these)

- 64 parks, Chiricahua included, with every fact verified against NPS.gov or Recreation.gov before it publishes.
- One-stop planner: itinerary builder, budget calculator, best-time and monthly visit score, quiet mode, drive times, packing, reservations and permits — plan, book, go.
- Travel guide: full park pages, gateway towns in layers, full guides near parks and short pages farther, weather with alerts, sunrise and sunset, moon and dark-sky, closures.
- After the Park: exit-based plans for what to do, eat and stay after leaving each park.
- Trust Layer: sources and verification dates shown, estimates labelled as estimates, FTC disclosure near every affiliate link.
- Chatbot that answers only from verified park data — never invents.
- Trip Collections, Collaborative Trips, Route Intelligence, Stay Smart, Seasons — the ten signature capabilities from the v2 blueprint.

## Rules for every model

- Study competitor sites for IDEAS only. Never copy their text, design, or structure wholesale. If a competitor does something well, beat it with verified data and a fuller answer — do not imitate it.
- Every feature you build must serve the one-stop goal: does it help a visitor plan, travel, or come back after the park? If not, it does not belong.
- Check your own work against this brief before marking it done: does this make Parkday more unique, or more like the others? If it makes us more like the others, say so on the board and propose the better version.
- Never lower our standard to match a competitor: no unverified facts, no invented prices, no copied text, no stale data.
