parkdayguide.com. 40 people (35 founders + 5 backup members), 7 days.

## 3. Rules that do not change

- From-scratch build only — the old 869-page base is not reused.
- Only the CEO authorizes publication. Nothing goes live without Hanuma's word.
- Launch-ready beats perfect, within the must-pass gates.
- Affiliate applications are prepared, not submitted, before launch. No affiliate
  link goes live before its program approves. No NPS fees or Recreation.gov
  links are ever affiliated.
- No FAQ rich results. No drone content — ground and local photos only.
- Photo use needs photographer permission; nothing publishes until it is cleared.
- Revenue forecast is a target, not a promise.

## 4. The six must-pass launch gates

A gate is red until its evidence is attached to the gate record on the shared
board. No gate is passed verbally. **All six green with evidence = launch can
proceed. Any red gate = NO-GO. No partial pass, no waiver.**

| Gate | Measure | Evidence | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|
