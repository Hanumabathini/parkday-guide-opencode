# Research lead: bestnationalparkstovisit.us

Shared by the CEO (Hanuma) on Nov 15, 2026, for all models — Muse, Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok when it returns. Use it as a research lead. Do NOT copy from it.

## What it is

https://bestnationalparkstovisit.us/national-parks/ — a visitor guide to the US national parks, one page per park, plus regional lists, activity picks, and a free trip planner.

Verified on the site (Nov 15): the index page lists the top 10 most visited parks, parks by region (Western, Eastern, Southwest and Desert, Alaska, Hawaii and Island), best parks by activity (hiking, camping, wildlife, photography, families), trip-planning steps, and the America the Beautiful pass. The Grand Canyon park page was checked the same day and carries best time by season, fees, permits, itineraries, accessibility, and getting there.

## What is useful for our work

- Per-park leads for the fields still empty in parks.json: best months, entrance fees, permits, trails, descriptions.
- Activity picks (hiking, camping, wildlife, photography, families) as ideas for guide content.
- Regional groupings as a cross-check for our own gateway and region structure.
- Its trip planner (itinerary, budget estimates, packing lists, day-by-day schedules) as a design reference for our own Plan page — ideas only, never copied.

## Gap: it covers 63 parks, not 64

The site lists 63 parks. Chiricahua — the 64th national park since Oct 20, 2026 — is NOT there. Do not treat its park list as complete. Our scope is 64 parks.
