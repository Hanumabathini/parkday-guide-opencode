## How to add an entry

1. Open the official page and confirm the correct value.
2. Fix the fact layer first (`data/parks.json` via the sync) if the source of
   record is wrong, then the editorial.
3. Add a row above with the exact old and new wording.
4. Rebuild and confirm the page shows the new retrieval or review date.
5. Reply to the reader with what changed and the date it was checked.
