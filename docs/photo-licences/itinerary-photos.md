# Itinerary photos (Smokies, Yosemite, Zion)

Checked 2026-11-19. Class: `nps-credit`. Every image is an NPS photograph hotlinked
from nps.gov and rendered with the credit the NPS page shows. No AI-generated
images, and no NPS arrowhead or other agency emblem, are used on any page.

The poster mockups the team made in ChatGPT (Yosemite, Zion, Great Smoky
Mountains) are used only as a layout reference. They are not published: they
contain generated scenes and the NPS arrowhead, a protected federal emblem, and
the Smokies mockup routes visitors to Laurel Falls, which is closed until 2027.
